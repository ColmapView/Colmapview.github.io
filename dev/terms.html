When you start a publication, you direct the app to transfer the dataset files and selected metadata to the chosen provider. The current Hugging Face publisher creates a new public dataset repository. Google Drive publication creates a private ZIP, with optional access for anyone with the link. Review the destination, license, and sharing choice before starting.

Public or link-accessible files may be copied by others. Cancellation, connection loss, or an error can leave provider-side files or repositories.
